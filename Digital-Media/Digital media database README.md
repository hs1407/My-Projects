# Chinook Digital Media Database Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Chinook digital media database to understand customer distribution, purchasing activity, music catalogue information, invoice trends, and sales-agent performance. The analysis connects customer, employee, invoice, track, album, artist, genre, and media-type data to create a clear view of the company’s sales operations.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform relational data into useful business information that can support decisions related to customer targeting, market performance, product demand, and sales-team effectiveness.

## Dataset Overview

The Chinook database represents a digital media store. It includes information about:

- Customers and their countries
- Employees and sales-support representatives
- Invoices and invoice items
- Tracks, albums, and artists
- Genres and media types

Together, these tables provide a structured view of the customer journey—from catalogue selection to purchase and sales support.

## Analysis Performed

### Customer and Geographic Analysis

- Identified customers located outside the United States
- Examined the customer base in Brazil
- Connected Brazilian customers with their invoice history
- Reviewed the countries represented in billing records

### Invoice and Revenue Analysis

- Connected invoices with the appropriate customers and sales agents
- Created a detailed view of invoice totals, customer information, country, and assigned representative
- Examined invoice volume during 2009
- Evaluated total sales generated during 2009

### Product and Purchase Analysis

- Matched purchased tracks with individual invoice-line records
- Connected purchased tracks with their corresponding artists
- Combined track information with album, genre, and media-type details

### Sales-Team Performance

- Identified employees working as sales-support agents
- Connected each sales agent with the invoices of their assigned customers
- Compared total sales generated by individual agents
- Evaluated which agent achieved the highest sales value in 2009

## Skills Demonstrated

- Relational database analysis
- Data filtering and sorting
- Combining information across multiple tables
- Aggregating invoice and revenue data
- Customer and geographic segmentation
- Product-level sales analysis
- Sales-performance evaluation
- Translating business questions into structured analysis

## Business Value

This analysis can help a digital media company:

- Understand where its customers are located
- Evaluate purchasing activity in specific markets
- Monitor invoice volume and revenue over time
- Connect sales results with individual representatives
- Explore how tracks, artists, albums, genres, and media formats relate to purchases
- Identify opportunities for more focused marketing and sales strategies

## Tools Used

- SQL

- Chinook sample database

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how a relational database can be used to connect customer, sales, employee, and music catalogue information. It provides a foundation for evaluating market activity, revenue performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and sales-team contributions in a digital media business.
